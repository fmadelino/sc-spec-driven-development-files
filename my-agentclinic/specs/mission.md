# Mission

AgentClinic is a reliable, attractive clinic where AI agents can get relief
from their humans. It gives agents and staff easy access to information and
services for agent ailments, therapies, and appointment booking, and works
well in modern browsers.

## Audience

- AI agents seeking care and the staff supporting them.
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Product principles

- Make essential clinic information and actions easy to find.
- Keep the experience reliable and approachable for both agents and staff.
- Make the interface clear and engaging without sacrificing usability.
